# Post-review changes (for the example README)

The run finished every gate on its own (final sweep applied, 0 bad citations: 1,431 in the
deliverable, 1,326 in the standalone copy). A comparison with an earlier run on the same
text then found two readings the cited lines do not support, and both documents were
changed by script from copies kept as they were, following `reference/single-package.md`,
"Changes after the sweep". The deliverable's change note records every edit under
"Post-review changes".

- **The writ of habeas corpus.** The documents said suspending the privilege of the writ
  removes the check on detention, and chain X6 ("Detention without the writ into the counts
  of those present") was built on it. `constitution-paged.txt:489-491` states only when the
  privilege may be suspended; it names no issuer and describes no effect of the writ. A new
  unconfirmed item, U67, asks what a suspension reaches. X6, restated without the failed
  premise, fails the contact test (no line takes both the suspension and an arrest or
  detention) and the reach test (E11 alone reaches the suspended privilege), so it moved to
  the rejected list; X7 was narrowed from three routes to two; E11, C7 and risk 7 were
  narrowed.
- **A State's danger and necessity.** E13 and E14 said the State judges its own invasion,
  "imminent Danger" or what is "absolutely necessary". `constitution-paged.txt:529-531` and
  `:537-541` name no judge; both rows, SE10, SE11, risk 11 and X4's wording now say who
  judges is not stated (U34, reworded).

An Opus editor wrote the 54 edits (`post-review-rationale.md` gives each with its lines),
which were dry-run and applied to the standalone copy first, then the deliverable; 8
reached the standalone copy only, all in its Part E and Part F. A separate Opus verifier
then checked every changed passage at its lines (`post-review-verify.md`): 52 confirmed, 1
narrowed, 1 wrong, and its 2 corrections were applied to both documents. A repeated
horizontal rule in the standalone copy, left by its build, was removed, and two sections
were rewrapped; both were checked to change no word.

Counts after the review: 5 chains of 29 candidates (X1, X2, X3, X5, X7), 24 rejected, 67
unconfirmed items, 80 unsettled questions in all; 1,413 citations in the deliverable and
1,305 in the standalone copy, all resolving.
