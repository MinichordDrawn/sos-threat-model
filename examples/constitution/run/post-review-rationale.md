# Post-review rationale

Two readings the cited lines do not support, corrected in both documents by
post-review-edits.md (54 edits, written against the standalone copy). The deliverable needs no
list of its own (post-review-edits-deliverable.md says why). Edits are referred to by number.

## The source lines

- `constitution-paged.txt:489-491` (p.11, Art. I §9 cl. 2): "The Privilege of the Writ of
  Habeas Corpus shall not be suspended, unless when in Cases of Rebellion or Invasion the
  public Safety may require it." The clause states when the privilege may be suspended. It
  names no body that suspends (U15, unchanged), no issuer of the writ, no person whose
  privilege it is, and no effect of the writ or of its suspension. No other line in the
  document describes the writ.
- `constitution-paged.txt:529-531` (p.12, Art. I §10 cl. 2): no State shall, without the
  Consent of the Congress, lay imposts or duties "except what may be absolutely necessary for
  executing its inspection Laws". `constitution-paged.txt:534-535` (p.12) subjects "all such
  Laws" to "the Revision and Control of the Congress". No line names who judges the necessity.
- `constitution-paged.txt:537-541` (p.12, Art. I §10 cl. 3): no State shall, without the
  Consent of Congress, "engage in War, unless actually invaded, or in such imminent Danger as
  will not admit of delay". No line names who judges either condition.

## Finding 1: the writ's effect

The documents read the suspension as removing "the check on detention" and built E11's impact
(detention of any person without the writ, the President and the members named), C7's wording,
risk 7, Part D entry 7, chain X6 and the custody route of chain X7 on it. Silence is not
absence: the lines state no effect, so whether a suspension removes any check is not settled
by the document. No existing U id asks it (U15 asks who suspends; U65 asks whether custody is
an inability), so U67 is added.

### Chain X6: the failed premise, the restatement and the test

- Failed premise: a suspension of the writ removes the check on detention, so that "the
  detention E11 permits" keeps members from the counts of those present (X6 steps 1 and 2, the
  join, the combined impact and the composition: "E11 and C7 (the suspension and detention no
  stated check reads)"). The lines at `constitution-paged.txt:489-491` (p.11) state no effect.
- Restatement, adding no premise in its place and keeping the gaps it joins: the writ

  "Treason, Felony and Breach of the Peace", which the privilege excepts

  or a treaty, as in X1's fourth step.
- Does a line put the ends in contact? No. The suspension clause takes no arrest or detention
  by name, and the privilege's line takes no writ; no other line joins them. Under "A failed
  premise rejects the premise, not the candidate", a step is not stated only where a line puts
  its two ends in contact; with no such line the gaps do not join. E11 does not feed the
  arrest step.
- What remains: an arrest on an excepted charge, which is left outside the privilege at the
  exclusion's own lines (SE2: no position gains; E35 dropped), feeding counts that are the

  that add are not a chain."
- What the lines say in place of the premise, tested as a gap: only the suspension by a body no
  line names, which is E11's own gap, already a row.
- Fate: the restatement fails, so X6 moves to the rejected list (edit 23, under the heading

## Finding 2: who judges a State's danger and necessity

The documents said a State reads its own exception ("on its own finding", "read by the State
itself", "the State's own finding"). The lines name no judge of either condition. U34 already
asked the question in a form that presupposed a State's finding ("Who checks a State's
findings of invasion or necessity"); it is reused, reworded to ask who judges the conditions
and to state that the lines name none (edits 38, 39). What settles it is unchanged: Congress's
revision as exercised for the inspection necessity, nothing in the document for invasion or
imminent danger.

- Edit 10, E13. Component: "a State's war on its own finding" becomes "a State's war under the
  exception", quoted from `constitution-paged.txt:540-541` (p.12). Threat: no line names who
  judges either condition; none is stated (U34). Impact: a State at war without consent where
  it is neither invaded nor in such danger, with no stated position judging the condition
  before the act. The entry stays the State (2), (3), whose act the line bars. Status: under
  "Silence is not absence" and "A stated exclusion is a stated absence", a judge not described
  is "none stated", covering not stated, an unconfirmed item, so the row stays conditional on
  U34. It does not become standing (no line states the absence of a judge) and is not dropped
  (the State gains war without consent wherever no judge refuses it).
- Edit 11, E14. Threat: no line names who judges whether an impost is "absolutely necessary
  for executing its inspection Laws"; none stated (U34); Congress's revision is after; the
  "net Produce" sentence unchanged. Impact narrowed the same way: imposts beyond what is
  "absolutely necessary" laid without consent, and their produce, with no stated position
  judging the necessity before the act. Status unchanged: conditional on U34, BC18 after.
- Edits 6 and 7, SE10 and SE11: "a bound read by the State itself" becomes "a bound whose judge
  the lines do not name (U34)". Dispositions unchanged (E13, E14).
- Edit 5, risk 11: the State's "own findings" sentence becomes "Who judges ... is not stated".
- Edit 28, Part D entry 11: "a State's war, imposts or compacts on its own finding or before
  consent" becomes "a State's war or imposts under an exception whose judge is not stated, or
  its compacts before consent".
- Edits 20 to 22, Part C, X4: X4 repeated the reading ("the State's finding is read by no stated
  position", "takes the State's own finding", "a State's finding of 'actually invaded'"). Each
  now names a judgment of the condition by no position the lines name. X4's verdict is
  unchanged: no line takes the condition, or any judgment of it, into the Union's protection.
- Not changed: OC13, the hazards row for OC13, DA16 and Part E's consents line cite U34 without
  saying who judges.
