# Living Things Under the Same Constraints

*A comparison, and not a result.* This page is the full comparison that the README
summarises under "Living Things".

Living things follow the model's four constraints. Within a tissue, nothing central
decides which cell lives. A missed cancer or infection can cost far more than a wasted
cell. A fault can look like health, as a cancer does. And nothing outside a solitary
organism watches it. Under those constraints, living things have arrived over time at
solutions similar to the model's.

The system in this comparison is the body, and not the cell. The scope of the system
extends outside the cell: a cell is one part, with a boundary of its own, and the immune
system watches it from outside that boundary and from inside the body. That is where the
model puts an observer. The same holds for a colony of bacteria, where dormant cells are
woken by their neighbours. Nobody watches a solitary body from outside. Some dependence on
another body remains: a human newborn, for one, relies on its mother's antibodies.

The model extends further than living things do. It closes the structure, so that the
observers are themselves observed. The ring is a fixed point of the question "who watches
this?": the answer is always a member already in it. The model's gate has no timeout and
no threshold. And the model always halts, where living things set the price of a false
alarm case by case.

A separate search went through the published biology one part of the model at a time. It
graded each counterpart, and looked for evidence against the comparison as well as for it.
Its grades, from closest to furthest, are *same structure*, *partial* and *loose*, and its
report does not define them further. **Each of the eight properties, the first eight rows
of the table, has a counterpart in living things: three with the same structure and five
in part.** The examples come from different organisms. A later check, described below,
asked the same of one kind of animal. The closed ring has no counterpart that the search
found.

Two parts of the model got the lowest grade. For the scope outward, the search found
failures and no counterpart. For the recursion, it found no coordinator that is a member
of a ring one level up.

**One animal.** The table draws on many organisms. A later check asked the same questions
of one kind of animal, the jellyfish. A moon jellyfish has no central controller. It
usually has eight pacemakers round the rim of its bell. Each fires by itself, and in one
account, which is disputed, each beat restarts the countdown of all the others, so that if
the leaders fall silent another fires in their place. In experiments reported in 1885,
with seven of the eight cut away, the last one kept the bell beating. That is close to the
liveness signal and to a member that starts first, in one animal, and the relation between
the pacemakers is the same as a watchdog timer's. The other half of the model is missing.
Nothing checks a pacemaker that is running wrong: the fastest one is simply obeyed. And
the animal does not stop on a fault. It swims on through damage, and in the young animal
the swimming is what does the repair. All eight properties have some counterpart in the
jellyfish, one with the same structure, five in part and two loosely, and none of those
counterparts observes faults. A moon jellyfish is covered against a silent pacemaker, and
not against a wrong one.

| No single-step removal | With seven of eight pacemakers removed, the last keeps the bell beating | Partial | The redundancy is of the starter, and not of observation. One cut round the rim removes them all |

**The ring.** The search found no closed ring: no three observers that all watch each
other, and no ring with one member different in kind. What it found is a guard on a guard,
two layers deep, and cells that are compared with their neighbours, with no standard of
soundness. The guard on a guard evolved separately in bacteria, in plants and in animals.
In bacteria the authors of one study describe it as "a second line of defense that is
triggered if the first lines of defense have collapsed". In every guard on a guard the top
layer is watched by nothing. The search for a loop among the immune system's regulators
was one of the checks that was stopped. The later check graded the jellyfish's pacemakers
as a ring in part: they restart each other and do not examine each other, and none is
different in kind.

Groups of animals were looked at as well, on thinner sources, which were the records of
the papers and not their full text. A typical wolf pack is a family led by its parents,
and packs often dissolved after a breeder was lost, most often when none remained. The
pair of parents is in part a member different in kind, but as a single point of failure
and not as part of a ring. Colonies of bees and ants would come nearest, if the unchecked
part holds. The queen gives off a signal that stops the workers reproducing. She does not
restart a halted colony, and the workers do not halt work. The rest of that comparison,
that workers police each other and raise a new queen when the signal stops, was not
checked.

**Cancer.** Cancer is the fault on the inward side of the scope, the kind that can pass
the checks. The gene for the damage protein p53 is the most often mutated gene in human
cancer. Tumours raise the signal that tells scavenger cells to leave a cell alone, and
some stop showing their proteins to the T cells. Of these three, the first removes an
observer, the second forges a signal of health, and the third hides the fault from a
check. Faults that pass are also common, and are often harmless: normal, sun-exposed skin
from the eyelid carries about 140 mutations of the kind that drive cancer in each square
centimetre, and still works.

Medicine answers cancer in the way the model says an owner can answer a fault its own
checks do not reach: with a check from outside, made at intervals. A screening is an
audit. It has the weakness the model gives every check of that kind. A cancer that arises
between two screenings, which doctors call an interval cancer, is the fault that lands
between two checks.

**The mind as a member.** One more reading is this page's own, checked afterwards
against standard physiology. The conscious mind is a member of the body's observers. It is
not their controller, and it is not outside them.

- *It shares the supply, and fails first.* When blood flow to the brain stops,
  consciousness is lost in about seven or eight seconds. The brainstem, which carries
  automatic functions, is the part of the brain that withstands a lack of oxygen longest.
- *It cannot switch another member off.* Heart rate is regulated without the will, and the
  immune system is regarded as beyond voluntary influence. Breathing, movement and emotion
  shift heart rate, and in one study training damped an immune response. None of these
  switches the function off. Breathing can be held for a time, and then an involuntary
  mechanism normally overrides the hold: people in normal health appear unable to hold
  their breath until they lose consciousness.
- *The other members can stop its work.* During an infection, signals from the immune
  cells act on the brain and bring on tiredness and withdrawal, though a pressing need can
  outweigh them. Sleep is the firmer case. After enough lost sleep, short lapses into
  sleep break in even when a person resists them.
- *The body calls on it.* On one standard view, pain is one of several signals, with
  hunger, thirst and itch, that ask the mind to act. It is not an exact report of damage,
  and lasting pain can go on with nothing to fix. People born unable to feel pain suffer
  repeated injuries and fractures, and their life expectancy is generally reduced.

The reading has limits. The body does have central control of its automatic functions, in
the brain, so its observers form layers under that control, and not a ring of equals. The
conscious mind is only not that control. The automatic layer overrides the mind only on
the few things it senses, such as the need to breathe, and that override can fail: after
over-breathing the urge to breathe comes late and consciousness can go first, which is
fatal under water. The mind is the one member that acts on the world outside the body. And
physiology does not treat "the conscious mind" as a unit, a point the check recalled and
did not look up. It speaks of voluntary and involuntary control.

**One scope up.** People also act as observers of each other, unevenly. Sociology treats
mutual regulation between spouses, in neighbourhoods and over shared resources as
ordinary. What happens without it is counted in large numbers, though that evidence only
describes and is moderate at best. In Japan in 2024, 76,020 people who lived alone died at
home. Of the 37,227 who died so in the first half of that year, about four in ten were
found by the next day, and about one in five after fifteen days or more. The watching is
real, and it is slow and patchy. The same check found no study that isolates being noticed
as the reason that people who are not alone fare better.

**More at stake, more observers.** Across 191 species of zoo mammal, deaths from cancer
are largely independent of body size and lifespan, which implies that larger species
defend themselves more. African elephants carry at least 20 copies of the p53 gene, 19 of
them processed copies of the gene, and their cancer mortality is estimated at about 5 in
100 against 11 to 25 in 100 for people. In rodents, larger species switch off an enzyme
that lets cells divide without limit. This is not one law. Bowhead whales rely on better
repair and not on more checks, the cells of long-lived bats turn cancerous after only two
mutations, and across land animals with backbones larger species do show more tumours,
though those that grew large fast have better defences.

**The cost of a false alarm.** The model's second constraint says that a halt that was not
needed is the cheap error. Living things set that price case by case. Mouse embryo cells
destroy themselves at small doses of radiation without first pausing to repair, and
elephant cells die more readily, on evidence that comes from blood cells. A check that
fires too readily has a cost all the same. A gene variant that removes a brake on blood
clotting raises the risk of clots in deep veins sevenfold, and mice with an overactive
fragment of p53 age early, though mice with one extra normal copy resist tumours and age
normally.

**Where the model extends further.** The search also looked for places where living things
contradict the model, and found these.

- *Halts time out.* Yeast stopped by a broken chromosome later divide anyway, and human
  cells slip out of a long arrest with the check still active.
- *Carrying on through a fault is standard.* Special low-accuracy enzymes copy past
  damaged DNA, so that copying continues with the damage in place.
- *A check can ignore small faults.* One human checkpoint lets cells divide with 10 to 20
  unrepaired chromosome breaks.
- *The line that matters most has the weakest gate.* Egg cells and early embryos run with
  some checks weak or not engaged. In one study, 21 of 23 early human embryos from
  fertility treatment were mosaics, with cells that differed from one another. The errors
  are removed later.
- *Living with a fault is a strategy.* A body can limit the damage of an infection and
  leave the pathogen alone.
- *Halted parts can do harm.* Clearing retired cells from a fast-ageing strain of mouse
  delayed its disorders.

Biology both halts and masks, and the search found no general rule for which. It found
only pieces. Cells that can be replaced halt, and so do cells whose death protects their
relatives. Cells that cannot be replaced mask, and a single cell living by itself carries
on.

**What the model covers.** The model covers every way of observing and stopping that the
search found in living things. Each mechanism in the table matches one or more parts of
the model, in whole or in part. The model has all the parts together, and the closure. Two
things that look like additions are already in it. Repair after a stop is the model's
recovery: the affected work stops, the fault is repaired or clears, and the work resumes.
A cell that stops for damaged DNA and later restarts does this, unless it restarts with
the damage still in place. Foresight is the gate with a wider scope. Take a self-driving
vehicle with a laser scanner. Built to the model, it moves only on a current reading that
the road ahead is clear, and it stops if the scanner goes silent. A mind that sees a
danger coming and stops for it does the same in part.

**What the model leaves out.** Apart from the places where living things contradict the
model, one thing they do has no part in it: changing the standard itself. An animal
learns, and evolution reworks the whole arrangement over generations. The model describes
a fixed system, with set kinds of fault. A fixed system can be safe without that ability,
and a lineage that has to keep up with a changing world cannot do without it. The result
that there is no gap holds for a given wiring, so a system that changes itself has to meet
the conditions again after each change. Growth is the part of this the model already has:
a new process or a new member takes effect only once it is being observed. A dividing cell
is held on a check in the same way only in part: that check leaks, and it is weak in egg
cells. A system built to the model would treat a change of standard as work too: it takes
effect only on a current check that the conditions still hold.

**What the comparison shows.** Living things follow the same constraints as the model and,
over time, arrived at similar solutions. They have a counterpart, in whole or in part, for
most of the model's parts, and none for its closure. The model extends that far beyond
them. The search found no living system that meets all four constraints, lacks one of the
properties and thrives. The nearest cases each break a constraint or pay a visible price.
An early embryo, for one, runs without some checks while it still lives on its mother's
supplies.

These are correspondences and not results. Nothing here is proved about biology, and the
biology is given in outline.
